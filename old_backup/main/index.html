01THE PERSON BEHIND THE VOICE

A creative practice rooted in feeling.

Kobir Sahabuddin (born 2 October 2002) is an Indian author, poet, Bengali voice actor, storyteller and creative content creator from West Bengal. His work moves between Bengali literature, poetry, voice acting, audio storytelling and digital content.

He writes about love, nostalgia, human emotion, relationships, silence and everyday life—then gives those worlds a second life through voice, sound and screen.
